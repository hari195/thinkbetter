<br/>

<span class="inline-link">[Peter Thiel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Thiel)</span> in his book <span class="inline-link">[Zero to One](https://www.amazon.com/Zero-One-Notes-Startups-Future/dp/0804139296)</span >, listed 7 questions a startup must answer in order to make it big.

<br>

1. __The Engineering Question__: Can the technology create a 10x improvement rather than an incremental improvement? Is the superiority in technology going to be obvious to the customer?
2. __The Timing Question__ : Why "now" is the right time for your startup and why is the problem you are solving important now? Is it a space that is trending up now? If yes, is it trending up slowly or fast?
3. __The Monopoly Question__ : Is the startup going to be a monopoly? Will it have a very very big market share in a small market that is growing? Will it be hard for new entrants to replicate what you have done?
4. __The People Question__ : Do you have the right people in your team? A team with a culture and conviction that can drive them to innovate such a solution into existence.
5. __The Distribution Question__ : Do you have a way to reach your customers efficiently? 
6. __The Durability Question__ : How can you dominate the market for the next 20+ years? What can fuel the consistent demand and supply? What will stop new entrants from putting you out of business over a long period of time?
7. __The Secret Question__ : Do you know something that others don't?
